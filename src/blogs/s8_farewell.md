# What I'm Leaving Behind (Besides Memories)

When Grace's husband passed, she inherited a small life insurance payout and a house that was already paid off. She loved her children and grandchildren deeply. But as she sat down to write her will, she realized something:

*Everything I own is either paper or digital.*

Stocks. Bonds. A savings account. A 401(k).

What could she *hand* to her grandchildren? What would they *feel*?

She remembered her own grandmother giving her a gold locket on her 16th birthday. She still had it. She still treasured it. No one could hack it. No bank could freeze it.

Grace decided to create a legacy that her family could *touch*.

She used a portion of her savings to buy physical silver — one custom-engraved bar for each grandchild, stamped with their name and birth year.

Then she joined the **Stack Squad™ — Platinum Tier** ($99.99/month) to keep adding.

*"I won't be here for their weddings,"* she says. *"But they'll open a box on that day and find a silver bar I bought years ago, thinking of them. That's a conversation across time. That's legacy."*

**The Lesson:** The most important thing you can leave your family isn't money. It's a piece of you that lasts.
