# I Season My Life With Silver

Elena is a line cook at a busy restaurant in New Orleans. She works 50-hour weeks. Her hands are scarred from the heat. Her paycheck is modest.

But she has a rule: **Every payday, she buys one ounce of silver.**

It started as a challenge to herself. She'd read the "10% Wealth Shield" article on Your Stack School™ and decided that if she couldn't save 10%, she could at least save something.

One ounce of silver — roughly $30–$35 depending on the spot price. Less than she'd spend on a single shift's worth of shift drinks and takeout.

*"People think you need to be rich to buy precious metals,"* Elena says. *"I make $18 an hour and I own more physical silver than most of my customers."*

She joined the **Stack Squad™ — Silver Tier** ($24.99/month) to automate it. The metal arrives like clockwork. She keeps her stack in a lockbox under her bed.

*"This is the only ingredient in my life that gets more valuable the longer I keep it. Everything else expires. Not this."*

Her goal: 100 ounces of silver by age 35. A financial security blanket that no landlord, no recession, and no restaurant closing can take away from her.

**The Lesson:** Consistency beats income. A line cook with a $25/month habit will have more real wealth in 10 years than a high earner who never starts.
